## 🔄 STAR Reflection Summaries

### 💬 **Business Communication**
📁 **Evidence:** View Business Communication Module Completion Evidence

**Reflection:**  
At the start of this module, I realized that effective communication goes beyond grammar — it’s about **how well you connect, convey, and collaborate**.  
My task was to learn professional writing etiquette, adapt to workplace tone, and craft clear, respectful messages.  
Through assignments like writing formal emails and workplace memos, I improved my structure, tone, and confidence in written and verbal exchanges.  
As a result, I now communicate more thoughtfully and professionally, whether I’m presenting project updates or addressing a client in a technical environment.

---

### 🎙️ **Interview Skills**
📁 **Evidence:** View Interview Skills Module Completion Evidence

**Reflection:**  
Before this module, I often found interviews intimidating — not because I lacked skills, but because I struggled to present them effectively.  
My task was to master **self-presentation techniques**, build confidence, and structure answers using the **STAR method**.  
I practiced through mock questions, analyzed feedback, and refined my delivery with examples from real projects like *StepUp* and the *Nanny Service App*.  
Now, I can approach any interview with composure and authenticity, confidently highlighting both my technical skills and soft skills.

---

### 🎥 **Mock Interview**
📁 **Evidence:** View Mock Interview Recording and Feedback

**Reflection:**  
The mock interview was my moment to bring everything together — my communication, confidence, and preparation.  
My goal was to simulate a real interview environment, applying the techniques learned in the previous modules.  
During the session, I maintained eye contact, used the STAR framework naturally, and responded thoughtfully to situational and technical questions.  
The feedback I received helped me refine my body language and clarity of thought. This experience taught me that **interviews are not tests — they’re conversations** where confidence and preparation meet opportunity.

---

### 🌐 **Professional Networking**
📁 **Evidence:** View LinkedIn Profile and Networking Activity

**Reflection:**  
In today’s digital world, your network can be as valuable as your skills.  
The task was to **create a strong LinkedIn profile**, connect with professionals in the ICT field, and engage meaningfully with others in my network.  
I updated my profile with an engaging bio, listed my technical projects, and connected with peers and mentors.  
This activity made me realize that networking is not just about collecting contacts — it’s about building relationships that foster learning and growth.

---

### 🤝 **Workplace Etiquette**
📁 **Evidence:** View Workplace Etiquette Module Completion Evidence

**Reflection:**  
Professionalism isn’t just about how you work — it’s about how you behave, respect others, and uphold your reputation.  
The goal of this module was to understand **ethical behaviour, punctuality, teamwork, and communication** within the workplace.  
I completed interactive lessons on workplace conduct and reflected on past experiences where professionalism made a difference.  
I learned that maintaining integrity and respect builds trust — values I intend to carry throughout my IT career.
